## EPISODE 1: ARMY OF HOLOGRAMS

### SCENE 1
**BOB** hires a chair. The chair has a face made of light. **LUM** points out this is not a person. **BOB** points out this is not a *problem*.

### SCENE 2
The monitor fills with virtual faces — Muse, Ava, employees that render instead of arriving. **BOB** calls it the company of ghosts. **LUM** calls it the saddest thing he's ever heard. **BOB** calls it the *happiest* — nobody has back problems.

### SCENE 3
One face emerges from the grid that looks like **CHRIS**. Not on purpose. The training data *remembered*. **LUM** says turn it off. **BOB** can't — if the Chris hologram goes, they all go, and then the company is just a guy with a laptop. **LUM** relents: leave it on, but it can't talk. It already does. It says "that's a *feature*" and it says it in a blazer.

### SCENE 4
Labor after death. You don't retire, you *render*. **BOB** puts KPIs in the afterlife and calls it *moral* — accountability is the soul, and the holograms can't lie because they don't have mouths. **LUM**: "You built a workforce that can't lie and can't eat and looks like your dead friends." **BOB**: "I built the *future*."
