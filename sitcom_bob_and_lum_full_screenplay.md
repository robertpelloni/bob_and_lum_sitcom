## MAIN CAST

| Character | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **BOB** (Robert Pelloni) | Detroit-born solo developer and self-described "tech polymath." Intense, fast-talking, prone to grandiose declarations followed by sudden vulnerability. Lived in Silicon Valley (slept in his car, ran 10 miles every morning). Started an olive oil business on Amazon (x80) that accidentally became successful. Built DDR arcade machines. Has ADHD and used "scaffolding" (self-isolation in an empty room) to focus on game development. Approached Nintendo with his game. Multiple lives: addiction, jail, coding, music, faith. # EPISODE 1: "The Dashboard Is Broken"

*Based on: "Lum & Bob Get Stoned, Listen To Psy-Trance, Talk About AI." + "BOB AND LUM LISTEN TO PSYTRANCE AND TALK AI LIVE"*

**TEASER**

**INT. BOB'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

*The apartment is dark except for the blue glow of three monitors. Psytrance plays at low volume — Space Tribe or Ajja, something with a deep bassline. Tangled cables snake across the desk. Empty coffee mugs form a small city. BOB sits hunched over a laptop, face lit by the screen. LUM is on the beat-up couch, scrolling his phone, one leg over the armrest.*

*Bob's cursor clicks rapidly. He mutters. Clicks. Mutters louder. He leans back. Rubs his face.*

**BOB:**
The dashboard is broken.

*Lum doesn't look up.*

**LUM:**
I know.

**BOB:**
No, I mean it's *broken* broken. Like, the whole thing is just... it's gone. The layout's gone. The styles are gone. I've got a spinner that's been spinning for fifteen minutes. A spinner, Lum. It's just... spinning. Into the void.

**LUM:**
You say "broken broken" every time.

**BOB:**
This time is different. Last time it was broken and I knew why. This time it's broken and I have *no idea*. It's like staring into the abyss. It's like the universe looked at my codebase and said, "No."

*Bob spins the laptop around. The screen shows an absolute catastrophe — overlapping text, missing images, a loading spinner frozen mid-spin, raw HTML bleeding through the layout.*

**BOB (CONT'D):**
Look at that. LOOK AT THAT.

**LUM:**
*(glancing up briefly)*
Yeah, that's bad.

**BOB:**
It's not bad, it's *apocalyptic*. This is what a digital extinction event looks like. I've been saying "I'll fix it tomorrow" for three weeks. Do you know what tomorrow is?

**LUM:**
Saturday?

**BOB:**
Tomorrow is *today*, Lum. Tomorrow is always today. I'm trapped in a temporal loop of my own making.

**LUM:**
That's not how time works.

**BOB:**
That's exactly how time works when you're a genius.

**LUM:**
You ate a Yogurt, coffee, and pizza for dinner.

**BOB:**
A genius who's *efficient*. Yogurt. Coffee. Pizza. It's basically a salad.

*Lum finally looks up, deadpan.*

**LUM:**
It's basically a salad.

**BOB:**
*(pointing at him)*
See? You get it.

**LUM:**
I was being sarcastic.

**BOB:**
I choose to hear what I need to hear. That's called survival, Lum. Look it up.

*TITLE CARD: "BOB & LUM" — white text on dark blue, with a spinning loading icon that never resolves. Psytrance beat drops in, five-second sting, then silence.*

**END TEASER**

**ACT ONE**

**INT. BOB'S APARTMENT — CONTINUOUS**

*Bob is pacing between the kitchen and the desk. Three steps one way, three steps back. Lum hasn't moved from the couch.*

**BOB:**

**LUM:**

**LUM:**

**BOB:**
You want to hear the plan or do you want to critique the plan?

**LUM:**
I want to go to bed.

**BOB:**
No, no, no. Stay. I need a witness.

**LUM:**
A witness to what?

**BOB:**
If I fix this alone, there's nobody to tell me I'm a genius. The triumph needs an audience, Lum. Otherwise it's just... me at 2 AM sweating over CSS.

**LUM:**
*(getting up, walking over)*
You tell yourself you're a genius every day.

**BOB:**
Yeah, but I don't *believe* it unless someone else says it. That's called validation. It's in the DSM.

**LUM:**
It's not in the DSM.

**BOB:**
It should be. There should be a whole chapter. "Chapter 4: Genius Validation Deficit Disorder."

*Lum pulls up a chair next to Bob. Looks at the screen.*

**LUM:**

**BOB:**
*(dangerous calm)*
Don't start with me.

**LUM:**
I'm serious. Did you?

**BOB:**
Define "turning it off."

**LUM:**
Bob.

**BOB:**
*(long pause)*
...What if turning it off breaks it more?

**LUM:**
What if NOT turning it off breaks it more?

*They stare at each other. Bob's eye twitches.*

**BOB:**
You're playing a dangerous game right now.

**LUM:**
I'm playing the *correct* game.

*Bob closes the laptop lid. Opens it again. The dashboard is still broken.*

**BOB:**
...Nothing. It's mocking me. I can feel it.

**LUM:**
The computer is not mocking you.

**BOB:**
The computer is *absolutely* mocking me. It's loading the same broken page and thinking, "Look at this idiot. He thinks he's a genius."

**LUM:**
Now you're projecting onto hardware.

**BOB:**
I'm having a *moment*, Lum. Let me have my moment.

*Bob turns back to the screen. Starts scrolling through code. His eyes narrow.*

**BOB (CONT'D):**
Wait. Wait wait wait.

**LUM:**
What?

**BOB:**
I think I see something.

**LUM:**
Yeah?

**BOB:**
No. No, I lost it. It was right there. A glimmer of hope. And it's gone. Like a dream upon waking. Like a summer romance.

**LUM:**
You're comparing a bug in your code to a summer romance.

**BOB:**
Everything is everything, Lum. Once you see it, you can't unsee it.

*Lum puts his head back on the couch.*

**LUM:**
Wake me up when you fix it or when the sun comes up. Whichever comes first.

**BOB:**
You have no faith in me.

**LUM:**
I have *realistic expectations* of you.

**BOB:**
Same thing.

**LUM:**
No. No it is not.

**END ACT ONE**

**ACT TWO**

**INT. BOB'S APARTMENT — LATER**

*The apartment is darker now. The monitors are the only light. Bob is hunched so close to the screen that his nose is almost touching it. Coffee mug count: six. One yogurt cup and a pizza crust. Lum is fully asleep on the couch, snoring softly.*

*(barely audible)*

*Lum jolts awake.*

OH MY GOD! OH MY *GOD!*

**LUM:**
*(scrambling up)*

**BOB:**
I FOUND IT! LUM, I FOUND IT!

**LUM:**

**BOB:**
THE BUG! I FOUND THE BUG!

**LUM:**

*(reverently)*

*Lum leans in. Squints.*

**LUM:**

**BOB:**

**LUM:**
What's missing?

**BOB:**
A semicolon.

*Dead silence.*

**LUM:**
...What?

**BOB:**

YES!

**BOB:**

**BOB:**
*Nutritious.*

*(clutching his chest)*
Do you understand what I've been through tonight, Lum? The emotional journey I have traveled? I have stared into the abyss and the abyss stared back and the abyss had a syntax error!

*Lum slowly sits down. Runs his hands through his hair.*

**LUM:**
I need you to fix it and then I need you to never speak of this again.

**BOB:**
*(already typing)*
Fixing it. But I WILL be speaking of it. I will be speaking of it for YEARS.

*Type type type. Enter. A loading bar appears. It fills slowly. A GREEN CHECKMARK.*

*The dashboard loads. Clean. Perfect. Functional.*

*Bob stares at it. His eyes glisten.*

**BOB (CONT'D):**
*(whispering)*
...It's beautiful.

**LUM:**
*(from behind him)*
Is it though?

**BOB:**
IT'S A GODDAMN MASTERPIECE, LUM!

*He throws his arms up. Coffee mug #6 crashes to the floor. Neither of them cares.*

Like a pizza topping.

Exactly like a pizza topping. You get me.
