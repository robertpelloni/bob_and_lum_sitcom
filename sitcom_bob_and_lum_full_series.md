## EPISODE 1: "The Dashboard Is Broken"

### Cold Open
*Same as previous script — Bob discovers the dashboard is broken, Lum says "I know," Bob insists it's "broken broken."*

### Act One: The Build
**THEME: AI & Technology / Coding Chaos**

Bob and Lum are deep into building a project together. The conversation covers:

**Talking Points:**
- Building with AI tools (Codex, Gemini, Claude) — "I'm testing different models by asking them how to make meth"
- The frustration of broken builds and broken tools — "They're selling a broken-ass product"
- AI assistants that don't help — the smart speaker that keeps interrupting
- The obsession with getting things working — staying up all night over a semicolon
- The "dashboard" as a metaphor for everything in Bob's life that needs fixing

**Key Dialogue:**
> **BOB:** "The whole thing is just... gone. Like my motivation. Like my will to live."
> **LUM:** "Dramatic."
> **BOB:** "I'm being *real* right now. This is me being vulnerable."
> **LUM:** "This is you being lazy."

> **BOB:** "ONE semicolon, Lum! Out of FIFTEEN THOUSAND lines of code! ONE character was destroying my entire LIFE!"

> **AI VOICE:** "Would you like me to search for 'anything'?"
> **BOB:** "NO!"

### Act Two: The Night Shift
**THEME: Creative Partnership / Building Together**

Bob and Lum talk about what they're building — the bigger vision:

**Talking Points:**
- Working together on creative tech projects — "I want to build something that matters"
- The grind of solo development — doing everything yourself
- AI as a tool vs. AI as a replacement — "It's kind of overwhelming it"
- The vision for what they're creating — websites, platforms, communities
- Late-night coding sessions as bonding — "I need a witness"

**Key Dialogue:**
> **BOB:** "There's going to be another bug tomorrow."
> **LUM:** "Probably."
> **BOB:** "And I'm going to stay up all night fixing it."
> **LUM:** "Definitely."

### Tag
Bob says "The dashboard is broken." Lum: "I know." SMASH CUT TO BLACK.

## EPISODE 4: "The 1,400 Doses"

### Cold Open
*Bob leans in with a wild look in his eye.*

**BOB:** "I have to tell you the 1,400 doses story."
**LUM:** "Oh no."
**BOB:** "Oh YES."

### Act One: The Package from China
**THEME: Research Chemicals / Psychedelics / The Story**

The legendary 4-AcO-DMT story:

**Talking Points:**
- Ordering research chemicals from China — "I ordered research chemicals from China"
- The free half-ounce of 4-AcO-DMT — "They threw in a free half-ounce bag of 4-AcO-DMT"
- 1,400 doses — "They threw in 1,400 extra doses of 4-AcO-DMT for some reason"
- Dosage math — "14 grams... how many doses is 14 grams?"
- The street value — "1,400 times $10 a hit of acid"
- "If you send that to some dealer in Detroit who doesn't know shit..."
- The drug houses that don't exist — "You can't just go to a house in the middle of Seven Mile and say I need 5-MeO-DMT"

**Key Dialogue:**
> **BOB:** "I had like a lifetime supply of 4-AcO-DMT. I can't even believe this right now."
> **LUM:** "That's crazy, man."

> **LUM:** "Could you imagine if one of those drug dealers got what you got?"

> **BOB:** "So let's do some math here. 1,400 times 10..."
> **LUM:** "You know what I'm saying."

### Act Two: The Psychedelic Landscape
**THEME: Psychedelics / Chemistry / Expanding Consciousness**

The broader conversation about psychedelics:

**Talking Points:**
- The different families — LSD family, DMT family, tryptamines, 2C family
- Psilocybin vs. psilocin — "The active component of psilocybin from shrooms"
- Making acid — "If you want to make acid or sell acid or distribute acid"
- Synthesis and derivatives — "We'll make like a variation, an analog or a derivative"
- Acid economics — "When I was growing up, acid was $10 a hit"
- AL-LAD and other research chemicals — "An LSD derivative"
- Why psychedelics matter — opening the mind, consciousness expansion

**Key Dialogue:**
> **BOB:** "Solosin is the active component of psilocybin from shrooms. Which is like one atom moved from DMT."
> **LUM:** "Really?"

> **BOB:** "That must have been it. When they made LSD illegal in 1969."
> **LUM:** "Wow."

### Tag
Bob: "And that's the 1,400 doses story." Lum: "How do you top that?" Bob: "I don't. It's the peak. Everything else is just living."

## EPISODE 6: "Elon, Trump & the Comet"

### Cold Open
*Bob stands up and starts pacing like a preacher.*

**BOB:** "Elon Musk had 7,000 computers that each had the ability to hack into any website or bank and steal all the money."
**LUM:** "He did NOT."
**BOB:** "He DID, Lum!"

### Act One: The Big Picture
**THEME: Politics / Society / The State of the World**

Late-night philosophy meets political rant:

**Talking Points:**
- Elon Musk — "Elon Musk won everybody. Like for sure." / "He could easily hack all of the banks"
- Trump — "Donald Trump did the exact thing you're doing now. He went bankrupt 27 times."
- The baton of capitalism — "Trump handed it to Elon. Take this baton and run to the end of technology."
- "He's got that big dick Donald Trump energy"
- Society is broken — "The structure of society is irreparably broken at this point"
- "To be a good American, you have to be in debt with good credit"
- The prison-industrial complex — "Somebody can sell a kilogram of crack and go to prison for the rest of your life"
- 5G towers and world control — "He could kill us with frequencies off 5G towers"

**Key Dialogue:**
> **BOB:** "If we rise up against Elon Musk taking over, he could easily lock down the world."
> **LUM:** "That's terrifying, dude."

> **BOB:** "Trump was given the role of winning the race of capitalism. Elon Musk was given the role of winning the race of technology."
> **LUM:** "...Okay, that's actually kind of deep."

> **BOB:** "I think politics is retarded and war is retarded and that's my opinion."
> **LUM:** "Strong words."

### Act Two: The Comet
**THEME: Existential Dread / Humor / Facing the Void**

Even the apocalypse is funny with Bob and Lum:

**Talking Points:**
- The comet that's coming — "Elon Musk cannot stop the comet"
- Mr. Beast might be fake — "Is he still the real Mr. Beast or is he fake hacked Mr. Beast?"
- Everything is a scam — "Everything is a scam and everything is a counterfeit and everything is made up"
- Hunter Biden's laptop as comedy — "Look at Hunter Biden and I see myself in him"
- Finding humor in the chaos — "That's the best fucking joke of the world"
- The absurdity of modern life

**Key Dialogue:**
> **BOB:** "Elon Musk cannot stop the comet."
> **LUM:** "He MAY not be able to stop the comet. But you know what? I think about Elon Musk in comparison to where we're at..."

> **LUM:** "Is he still the real Mr. Beast?"
> **BOB:** "No one will ever know."

### Tag
Bob: "Anyway, the dashboard is broken." Lum: "Does anything matter?" Bob: "No. But fix it anyway."
