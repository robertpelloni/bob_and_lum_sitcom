## CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEETS

**BOB (Robert Pelloni):** 43. Gray hair coming in. Wears whatever his mom dressed him in — at 43. Only a couple days in jail. One very long phone call about Shonna's foreclosure. Hoodie up. Glasses slightly crooked. Eyes slightly too wide — "the look of a man who has seen the dashboard and it is broken." Posture: hunched over a single keyboard. Expressions cycle between manic glee and thousand-yard stare. Detroit accent leaking through. Sometimes holds a slice of stale pizza like a cigarette.

**LUM:** 44. Korean mother + Greek father — mixed features. Dark hair, no gray yet. Clean hands. Deadpan eyes that have seen too much and drawn even more. Posture: slouched into a couch like it owes him money. Sometimes smoking. Sometimes holding a honey bun. A genius artist in every sense — has been to prison AND jail — sketching on napkins, receipts, walls, the margins of the universe. Voice is low, dry, occasionally cracks into warmth.

**SETTING — THE APARTMENT:** Small Michigan apartment. ONE monitor at a desk in the center of an otherwise empty room (Bob's "scaffolding"). A laptop — used only at Lum's place. LED strips along the ceiling. Psytrance posters (Ajja, Space Tribe, 1200 Micrograms, Jumpstreet). A whiteboard covered in arrows and words like "SUPERNOVA" and "GLUE" and "THE FIRE IS THINKING." Greasy pizza boxes stacked like a shrine to bad decisions. Honey bun wrappers. No smart speaker — never was. A phone on the counter with a faint glow.
