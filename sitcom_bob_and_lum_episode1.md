# BOB & LUM
## Episode 1: "The Dashboard Is Broken"
### A Sitcom Script

---

> **HISTORICAL CUT (canon applied 2026-10-13).** The authoritative rewrites are `sitcom_final_s1.md` (12 eps) and `sitcom_final_s2.md` (8 eps + 6 bonus), plus `sitcom_rewrite_s2.md` (writers'-room cut). This file keeps its original episode taxonomy for reference. Canon held: Bob eats yogurt/coffee/pizza (never Pop-Tarts); Noah died next to Lum; Chris died for us both; Bob was on shrooms with Chris attacking Nintendo for no reason; Lum said "we are the perfect balanced brain."


**SERIES:** Bob & Lum
**EPISODE:** 1 — "The Dashboard Is Broken"
**RUNTIME:** ~22 minutes (half-hour sitcom)
**GENRE:** Stoner buddy comedy / Tech satire
**SETTING:** A cluttered Detroit-area apartment filled with monitors, tangled cables, and takeout containers. Psytrance plays faintly in the background at all times.

---

### MAIN CAST

| Character | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **BOB** (Robert Pelloni) | 43, Detroit-born solo developer and self-described "tech polymath." Intense, fast-talking, prone to grandiose declarations followed immediately by self-deprecation. Wears the same hoodie every episode. |
| **LUM** | Bob's best friend and creative partner. Calm counterweight to Bob's chaos. Dry wit. Often the voice of reason that nobody listens to. |

### RECURRING

| Character | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **THE DASHBOARD** | A broken web interface that Bob insists he's "about to fix" every episode. |
| **THE AI** | Various AI assistants that talk back through speakers — sometimes helpful, usually not. |

---

### COLD OPEN

**INT. LUM'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

*Monitors glow blue in a dark room. Psytrance thumps at low volume. BOB stares intensely at a laptop. LUM sits on a beat-up couch, scrolling his phone.*

**BOB:**
The dashboard is broken.

**LUM:**
I know.

**BOB:**
No, I mean it's *broken* broken. Like, I've been saying "I'll fix it tomorrow" for three weeks, and now tomorrow is yesterday's today.

**LUM:**
That's not how time works.

**BOB:**
That's exactly how time works when you're a genius.

**LUM:**
You ate a Yogurt, coffee, and pizza for dinner.

**BOB:**
A genius who's *efficient*, Lum. Yogurt, coffee, and pizza — the holy trinity.

*Bob spins his laptop around to show a web page that is clearly, catastrophically broken — overlapping text, missing images, a spinner that won't stop loading.*

**BOB (CONT'D):**
Look at this. The whole thing is just... gone. Like my motivation. Like my will to live.

**LUM:**
Dramatic.

**BOB:**
I'm being *real* right now. This is me being vulnerable.

**LUM:**
This is you being lazy.

**BOB:**
...That's also fair.

*TITLE CARD: "BOB & LUM" with a spinning loading icon that never resolves.*

**[END COLD OPEN]**

---

### ACT ONE

**INT. BOB'S APARTMENT — KITCHEN AREA — CONTINUOUS**

*Bob paces between the kitchen and the desk area. Lum hasn't moved.*

**BOB:**
Okay, so here's the plan. I'm gonna open the codebase, find the bug, fix it, deploy it, and then I'm gonna sleep for twelve hours.

**LUM:**
What time is it?

**BOB:**
*(checking phone)*
...It's after midnight.

**LUM:**
So the twelve hours of sleep is already a lie.

**BOB:**
It's aspirational.

*Bob sits down, opens his laptop. Immediately, a terminal window fills with red error text.*

**BOB (CONT'D):**
Oh, great. Now the *build* is broken too.

**LUM:**
Was it working before?

**BOB:**
Define "before." And define "working."

**LUM:**
I'm going to bed.

**BOB:**
No, no, no, stay. I need a witness. If I fix this alone, there's nobody to tell me I'm a genius.

**LUM:**
You tell yourself that every day.

**BOB:**
Yeah, but I don't *believe* it unless someone else says it. That's called validation, Lum. Look it up.

*Lum sighs, gets up, and walks over to look at the screen.*

**LUM:**
...Did you try turning it off and on again?

**BOB:**
Don't start with me.

**LUM:**
I'm serious. Did you?

**BOB:**
*(long pause)*
...Define "turning it off."

---

**INT. BOB'S APARTMENT — LATER**

*Both are now at the desk. Multiple browser tabs are open. The dashboard is still broken. An AI voice speaks from a smart speaker.*

**AI VOICE:**
I'm sorry, I didn't understand that.

**BOB:**
I didn't even ask you anything!

**AI VOICE:**
Would you like me to search for "anything"?

**BOB:**
NO!

**LUM:**
*(laughing)*
Dude, even the AI is messing with you.

**BOB:**
This whole universe is conspiring against me, Lum. I'm telling you. The government, the AI, the dashboard... they're all in on it.

**LUM:**
You think the *government* broke your dashboard?

**BOB:**
I think the government broke my *spirit*. The dashboard just followed.

**LUM:**
*(beat)*
Okay, that's actually kind of deep.

**BOB:**
See? I have those. I have deep moments.

**LUM:**
Between yogurt, coffee, and pizza dinners.

**BOB:**
The pizza was *multigrain*. ...That's not a thing. The pizza was *pizza*.

---

**INT. BOB'S APARTMENT — EVEN LATER — THE BREAKTHROUGH**

*Bob is hunched over the keyboard. Empty coffee cups litter the desk. Lum is half-asleep on the couch.*

**BOB:**
OH! Oh my god. Oh my GOD!

**LUM:**
*(jolting awake)*
What?! What happened?!

**BOB:**
I found it! I found the bug!

**LUM:**
No way! Where was it?!

**BOB:**
It was... it was a missing semicolon.

**LUM:**
...

**BOB:**
...

**LUM:**
A semicolon.

**BOB:**
ONE semicolon, Lum. ONE character. Out of FIFTEEN THOUSAND lines of code. ONE character was destroying my entire LIFE.

**LUM:**
*(lying back down)*
You know, for a genius, you sure do—

**BOB:**
Don't. Don't say it. Don't ruin this moment.

**LUM:**
I was going to say "inspire me."

**BOB:**
No you weren't.

**LUM:**
No I wasn't.

*Bob types furiously. A loading bar appears... and fills. A green checkmark. The dashboard loads correctly.*

**BOB:**
*(whispering)*
...It's beautiful.

**LUM:**
*(from the couch)*
Is it though?

**BOB:**
IT'S A GODDAMN MASTERPIECE, LUM!

---

### ACT TWO

**INT. BOB'S APARTMENT — THE NEXT MORNING**

*Sunlight streams in. Bob is asleep at the desk, face on keyboard. Lum enters with coffee.*

**LUM:**
Hey. Hey, Bob. Wake up.

**BOB:**
*(lifting face, keyboard imprints on forehead)*
Mmrph. Did I fix it?

**LUM:**
Yeah, you fixed it. You also typed "JJJJJJJJJJJJ" into the commit message and deployed to production.

**BOB:**
*(sitting up fast)*
I WHAT?!

**LUM:**
I'm kidding. I caught it before it pushed.

**BOB:**
*(clutching chest)*
Don't DO that! My heart can't take it! I'm forty-two! I'm an old man!

**LUM:**
You had yogurt, coffee, and pizza for dinner and stayed up until 4 AM fixing a semicolon. You're basically a teenager.

**BOB:**
A teenager with *lower back pain*.

*Lum hands him the coffee. Bob takes it, stares into it like it holds the meaning of life.*

**BOB (CONT'D):**
You know what the worst part is?

**LUM:**
What?

**BOB:**
There's going to be another bug tomorrow.

**LUM:**
Probably.

**BOB:**
And I'm going to stay up all night fixing it.

**LUM:**
Definitely.

**BOB:**
And I'm going to have more yogurt, coffee, and pizza.

**LUM:**
Yogurt?

**BOB:**
...Obviously. And coffee. And pizza.

*They clink coffee mugs. The smart speaker chimes.*

**AI VOICE:**
Would you like me to order more yogurt, coffee, and pizza?

**BOB:**
...How does it KNOW?!

**LUM:**
*(laughing)*
Dude, it's always listening.

**BOB:**
*(to the speaker)*
I am a GENIUS and I will NOT be judged by a SPEAKER!

**AI VOICE:**
Noted. Adding "genius" to your shopping list.

*Bob buries his face in his hands. Lum laughs harder. Freeze frame.*
