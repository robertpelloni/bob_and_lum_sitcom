# S1E1 "Yogurt, Coffee, and Pizza" — Clip Shot List

Source: sitcom_final_s1.md (repo robertpelloni/bob_and_lum_sitcom @ f1260543), Episode 1, lines 52-200. Dialogue is verbatim from the canonical script. 18 clips x ~8s (Veo) ≈ 2.4 min + locally-made title card (no generated text).

## E1-01 — TEASER (visual)

Wide shot, 3:47 AM. Bob sits in blue monitor glow eating yogurt directly from the container with a FORK, not a spoon. Behind him a broken computer dashboard glows red. Lum enters holding a coffee mug, looks at Bob, unimpressed. No dialogue in this clip, only room tone and faint psytrance music.

Dialogue: (none)

## E1-02 — TEASER (dialogue)

Two-shot on the couch area. Lum, holding his coffee mug, states it flatly. Bob corrects him without looking up from the yogurt. Lum delivers the punchline deadpan.

Dialogue: LUM: It's four in the morning.
BOB: It's three forty-seven.
LUM: That's worse. That's the official wrong answer.

## E1-03 — TEASER (dialogue)

Bob holds up the generic yogurt container toward Lum like evidence; the label is half torn. Lum stares at it, horrified and flat.

Dialogue: BOB: This is my dinner.
LUM: That's a crime.

## E1-04 — TEASER (dialogue)

Bob delivers a small sermon, gesturing with the fork, yogurt container raised like a chalice. Lum watches, deadpan, sipping coffee.

Dialogue: BOB: Yogurt, coffee, and pizza. The holy trinity. Breakfast of champions and also dinner of men who have given up on the concept of meals.

## E1-05 — ACT ONE (dialogue)

Kitchen. The counter is a museum of leaning pizza boxes forming a small skyline; a single yogurt cup stands among them like a lighthouse. Lum lectures; Bob pushes back.

Dialogue: LUM: You know there's a system. You eat the pizza first.
BOB: I eat the pizza last.

## E1-06 — ACT ONE (dialogue)

Kitchen two-shot. Lum counts the order on his fingers; Bob interrupts, offended; Lum answers with total certainty.

Dialogue: LUM: Then you eat the coffee—
BOB: You don't eat the coffee.
LUM: You do. I've watched you.

## E1-07 — ACT ONE (dialogue)

Lum explains the system like a religion, pointing from coffee mug to yogurt cup to pizza box in turn. Bob listens with growing gravity.

Dialogue: LUM: You make it so strong it becomes a food group. You eat the coffee, then the yogurt, then the pizza is the ending. The pizza is the credits.

## E1-08 — ACT ONE (dialogue)

Bob receives the idea like a revelation, repeats it. He demands it be written down; Lum refuses flatly; Bob declares the show will write it down, looking vaguely toward camera.

Dialogue: BOB: ...The pizza is the credits.
LUM: The pizza is the credits.
BOB: Write that down.
LUM: I'm not writing that down.
BOB: Then the show will write it down.

## E1-09 — ACT ONE (dialogue)

A small smart speaker on the counter lights up and speaks. Both men freeze and stare at it. Bob is spooked; Lum is resigned; Bob lands the distinction like a verdict.

Dialogue: AI VOICE (cheerful smart-speaker voice): Would you like me to order more yogurt, coffee, and pizza?
BOB: ...How does it KNOW?
LUM: Dude. It's always listening.
BOB: It's always understanding. That's different. That's worse.

## E1-10 — ACT TWO (dialogue)

Bob pivots to the monitor: six red error panels, one corner playing looping psytrance visuals. Bob is messianic at the keyboard; Lum, on the couch with a coffee mug, punctures it; Bob reframes it as poetry.

Dialogue: BOB: Fix the dashboard and you fix the company. Fix the company and you fix the world.
LUM: Fix the dashboard and it breaks again at noon.
BOB: That's not a bug. That's a heartbeat.

## E1-11 — ACT TWO (dialogue)

Close on the monitor as Bob types: the error text changes from RED to a DIFFERENT shade of RED. Lum, dry: Progress. Bob, proud: Meaningful progress.

Dialogue: LUM: Progress.
BOB: Meaningful progress.

## E1-12 — ACT TWO (dialogue)

A pizza box slides off the stack and lands open, one cold slice inside like a letter from a past self. Both men look down at it. Lum does the math out loud, unbothered.

Dialogue: LUM: That's yours. That's the one from Tuesday.
BOB: It's Thursday.
LUM: So it's from two Tuesdays ago.

## E1-13 — ACT TWO (dialogue)

Bob picks up the cold slice and holds it like a prosecutor's pointer, delivering an intense, sincere speech. Lum listens, caught off guard by the ending.

Dialogue: BOB: This is my inheritance. Not the Corvette. Not Papu's business. This. Cold pizza. A dashboard that won't compile. And you.

## E1-14 — ACT TWO (dialogue)

Quiet beat. Lum asks it softly, almost moved. Bob answers simply, no joke in his voice. Hold on both of them a moment.

Dialogue: LUM: ...I'm your inheritance?
BOB: You're the dashboard, Lum. You're the thing that's always broken and always here.

## E1-15 — ACT THREE (dialogue)

Later, 4:22 AM, dawn starting. Both men on the couch, two coffee mugs, one empty yogurt container, one open pizza box. The broken dashboard glows behind them. Lum muses; Bob, flat, asks what show.

Dialogue: LUM: You ever think about how this is the whole show?
BOB: What show.

## E1-16 — ACT THREE (dialogue)

Slow push-in on the couch. Lum gestures around the room at each item as he names it. Bob corrects him gently. Lum closes it, quiet and certain.

Dialogue: LUM: This one. The two of us. The food. The broken thing on the screen. The psytrance at four in the morning. It's a format.
BOB: It's our lives.
LUM: Same thing. It was always the same thing.

## E1-17 — ACT THREE (dialogue)

Bob suddenly gets up and paces, energized, laying out the format with his hands. Lum watches from the couch and confirms it. Bob stops, triumphant: that's the whole show.

Dialogue: BOB: Yogurt, coffee, and pizza. Open on the food. Close on the dashboard. Everything in between is just... the two of us talking.
LUM: That's the show.
BOB: That's the whole show.

## E1-18 — ACT THREE (dialogue)

Final shot: hold on the two men and the broken dashboard glowing behind them, cold slice in the open box, psytrance faint. They repeat the line together like a ritual as the scene settles to stillness. End of episode.

Dialogue: LUM: Don't order the pizza yet. Eat the yogurt first.
BOB: The pizza is the credits.
LUM: The pizza is the credits.
